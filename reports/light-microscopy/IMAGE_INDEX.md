## 2026-11-10 — after 6 additional water washes

Scope: Dino-Lite AM4117 (per the operator's notes), DinoCapture 3.0.

The further rinses before imaging on Nov 10 were six rinses in a 24-well plate, each with
1 mL ultrapure water (Invitrogen UltraPure Distilled Water, DNase/RNase free, REF 10977-015,
LOT 2908108; product identified from the lab bottle, photographed Nov 12), 30–60 seconds
incubation per rinse (Will Clem's notes, gFET Matrix room, Nov 10). Then re-imaged under white and blue light. Channel map from the same notes:
A0036 white excitation, low magnification; A0037 blue excitation, same area;
A0038 white excitation, higher magnification of a corner; A0039 blue excitation, same area.
A0037 and A0039 are dark blue-excitation captures of chips with no fluorophore applied.
They document the appearance of the no-probe background under the acquisition conditions used.
Their dark appearance does not establish zero optical background, a detection limit, or the
absence of all fluorescence.

## Notes and deviations

Process coverage in this repo: [PBASE coating, Nov 4](../../protocols/2026-11-04-pbase-dmf-graphene.md),
[amino-GATTACA attachment and blocking, Nov 5](../../protocols/2026-11-05-amino-gattaca-pbase-graphene.md),
and [water rinsing and Dino-Lite imaging, Nov 10](../../protocols/2026-11-11-water-rinse-and-dino-lite-imaging.md).
The following notes accompany the images.

**2026-11-09 session**
- The excitation setting for A0001–A0011 remains unconfirmed. These captures are retained as
  no-probe background records without assigning a green or blue excitation channel.
- Capture settings (exposure, gain, white balance, magnification) were not recorded for any
  Nov 9 frame. Deviation: future sessions are to use fixed manual settings, recorded in the
  run record (see the cautions in the repo rinse record).

**2026-11-10 session**
- Date: the rinse and imaging session ran Nov 10.
- Further rinses before imaging: the additional rinses were the six 1 mL rinses
  recorded above, each 30–60 seconds in ultrapure water. The operator's Matrix
  session note ("Imaging after additional rinses") is the source of the A0036-A0039
  channel map above.
- Rinse technique for the Nov 10 session, from the operator's bench photos and caption (Matrix): washing was
  done in a 24-well plate, with the pipet tip landing in the space between the edge of the
  square chip and the wall of the round well.
- Capture settings: the historical settings were not recorded. Going forward, the settings will be recorded on the settings record form below.
- Settings record form: [DinoCapture settings record](../../protocols/dinocapture-settings-record.md) — the form for recording the microscope capture settings as they are determined (trial settings and the decided lab standard), kept in protocols/ in this repo.
- Storage after the Nov 10 session: the samples were stored in the lab desiccator under vacuum.
  Foil wrapping has not yet been performed. After fluorophore application, the plan is to wrap
  the samples in foil before storage in the vacuum desiccator.
