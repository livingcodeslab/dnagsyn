# PBASE in DMF: graphene coating

Solution preparation + 100-minute deposition | 4 November 2026

**Recipe:** approximately 5 mM PBASE in dry DMF; 500 µL batch. For an exposed, bare graphene chip, approximately 1 cm². Endpoint: rinsed and dried for AFM.

Next step: [first attachment of amino-gattaca to PBASE-functionalized graphene](2026-11-05-amino-gattaca-pbase-graphene.md) (DNA coupling and ethanolamine blocking).

## 1. Prepare and mix

**Reagents:** 0.964 mg PBASE (MW 385.41 g/mol) + 500 µL dry DMF.

1. Weigh PBASE directly into a small, clean, dry glass vial. Add 500 µL dry DMF with a micropipette.
2. Cap and gently swirl or briefly vortex until dissolved. Prepare fresh for this experiment. Mix in the vial, not on the graphene.

**Practical rounding:** 1.00 mg in 500 µL gives 5.19 mM, acceptable for this starting coating experiment. Adding 500 µL solvent rather than adjusting to a final volume has negligible effect here. Use a balance capable of weighing around 1 mg reliably.

## 2. Deposit on graphene

3. Place the chip level, graphene-side up, in a clean solvent-compatible container. Transfer approximately **100-200 µL** solution onto the exposed graphene, enough to cover the intended area.
4. Cover the container and incubate for **100 minutes (12:30-2:10 pm) at room temperature** in the fume hood. **Keep the coated area wet throughout.** Do not let the droplet dry onto the surface.

## 3. Rinse and dry for AFM

5. Immediately rinse with **three fresh 100 µL portions of DMF (100 µL per rinse)**, approximately **30-60 seconds per rinse**. Handle gently and do not touch the graphene.
6. With the chip on a **Kimwipe**, follow with **three brief ultrapure-water rinses, using 1 mL per rinse**, then gently dry with nitrogen. This is the AFM endpoint; DNA coupling is not included.

## Handling notes

Use a regular micropipette with a fresh polypropylene tip for brief DMF transfers. Dispense promptly and keep solvent out of the pipette body. This procedure assumes exposed graphene; compatibility of packaged-device resin with DMF has not been established.
